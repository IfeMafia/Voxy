1. What Is Voxy?
Voxy is an AI employee for businesses. It combines a customer-facing AI agent with a business operations
layer so a company can communicate with customers, sell products or services, process orders, facilitate
payments, and keep the owner informed.
The key difference from a traditional chatbot is that Voxy is designed to take action. It should understand what
a customer wants, use the business's approved information and tools, confirm important details, execute the
appropriate action, and keep the business updated.
The product can be understood as two connected sides:
●
Customer Side: the AI employee interacts with customers, answers questions, recommends products, sells,
confirms orders and supports payment.
●
Business Side: the owner or staff manage products, prices, policies, orders, customers, payments,
inventory information and agent settings.
Core promise
 Voxy helps businesses sell more, respond faster and automate repetitive operations
without requiring a large customer-service or operations team.
2. The Problem Voxy Solves
Many small businesses depend heavily on the owner or a small team to answer customer questions,
recommend products, take orders, follow up, confirm payments and coordinate daily tasks.
●
 Customers may wait too long for a response and buy elsewhere.
●
 Sales enquiries are not consistently followed up.
●
 Staff repeatedly answer the same questions.
●
 Orders can be misunderstood or recorded incorrectly.
●
 Business owners lack a simple view of conversations, orders and operational issues.
●
 Customers increasingly expect fast, conversational and convenient support.
Voxy addresses this by turning the business's existing information and workflows into an AI-powered employee
that can operate continuously and hand off sensitive or complex situations to a human.

6. The Customer-Facing AI Employee
The customer-facing side is where Voxy behaves like a trained sales and customer-service employee. It should
not respond with generic AI language. Its behavior should be grounded in the business catalogue, business
policies and conversation context.
Expected conversation behavior
●
Understand intent: identify whether the customer wants information, a recommendation, an order, a
complaint, a status update or another action.
●
Ask only useful questions: collect the minimum information needed to make a good recommendation or
complete an order.

●
Recommend: use customer needs, preferences, budget and catalogue data to recommend relevant
products.
●
Sell naturally: explain value, handle common objections and offer relevant add-ons without being pushy.
●
Remember the conversation: avoid asking for information the customer has already provided.
●
Stay truthful: never invent stock, prices, discounts, delivery times or policies.
●
Escalate: hand complex, sensitive or unsupported cases to a human.
7. Order Confirmation & Accuracy
A critical part of the agentic experience is confirmation before an irreversible or financially important action.
Example
Customer: “I want two Family Jollof Combos with chicken and five bottles of Coke.”
Voxy: “Just to confirm, you’d like 2 Family Jollof Combos with chicken and 5 bottles of Coke. Your total is
■XX,XXX, excluding delivery. Is that correct?”
Customer: “Yes.”
Only after confirmation should Voxy proceed to the next permitted action, such as generating a payment
request or creating the final order.
8. Payments
Payments should be treated as a business action performed through a trusted payment provider rather than
something the AI itself handles insecurely.
Target flow
●
 Voxy confirms the order and amount.
●
 Voxy creates or requests a payment link through the connected payment provider.
●
 The customer completes payment on the provider's secure checkout.
●
 Voxy receives a verified payment status through the integration.
●
 The order is marked paid.
●
 The business dashboard is updated.
●
 The customer receives confirmation.
For an MVP, integrate one payment provider first. The architecture should allow additional providers later.

10. Agentic AI — How Voxy Makes Decisions
Voxy should be designed as an agent, not only a conversational model. The AI receives an objective,
understands the current state, chooses an allowed action, uses a business tool, observes the result, and
continues until the task is completed or requires human intervention.
Example agent loop
Customer request → Intent detection → Context retrieval → Decision → Tool/action → Result → Next
step → Confirmation or completion → Record activity
Example
●
 Customer wants to order food.
●
 Voxy checks the product catalogue.
●
 Voxy recommends an appropriate package.
●
 Customer selects items.
●
 Voxy summarizes the order.
●
 Customer confirms.
●
 Voxy creates the order.
●
 Voxy requests a payment link.
●
 Payment provider confirms payment.
●
 Voxy updates the order and notifies the business.
●
 Voxy tells the customer the order is confirmed.
The agent should have explicit tool permissions. For example, it may be allowed to create an order and
generate a payment request, but it should not be allowed to issue refunds or change prices unless the business
has explicitly enabled that capability.
11. Making Voxy Sound Like a Real Sales Employee

strong sales representative while remaining accurate and consistent.
●

●
Understand customer intent before responding.

12. Voxy Voice
Voxy Voice extends the same AI employee experience to spoken conversations. A customer can speak to
Voxy, and Voxy can understand the request and respond using voice.
Basic voice flow
●
 Customer speaks.
●
 Speech is converted to text / interpreted by the voice system.
●
 Voxy determines intent and retrieves the required business information.
●
 The agent decides what to say or what action to take.
●
 The response is converted to natural speech.
●
 Customer continues the conversation.
The same business rules, product data, order tools and payment workflow should be used for voice and text.
Voice should be an additional interface, not a separate business brain.
13. Multilingual Experience
Voxy retains its original multilingual capability as part of the new AI employee experience. Customers should be
able to communicate in supported languages while the business maintains one centralized catalogue and
workflow.
●
 Detect or allow the customer to select a language.
●
Respond in the customer's preferred language.
●
Keep product names, prices and business rules consistent across languages.
●
Preserve the same sales and order-confirmation behavior regardless of language.

14. Human Handoff & Safety
An AI employee should know its limits. Voxy must provide a clear route to a human whenever the AI cannot
reliably complete the request.
●
Customer requests a human.
●
AI lacks required information.
●
Customer has a complex complaint.
●
Refund or other sensitive financial action requires approval.
●
The requested product or service is unavailable or ambiguous.
●
The system detects a potential error or conflicting business information.
Every important agent action should be logged so the business can review what happened.
15. Suggested End-to-End Example
Business: Mama Kitchen's Food
Customer: “Hi, I need food for 6 people tonight.”
Step 1 — Understand: Voxy recognizes a purchase intent and a party size.
Step 2 — Recommend: It suggests the best matching menu options.
Step 3 — Customize: It asks about relevant preferences only.
Step 4 — Add-ons: It offers relevant drinks or sides.
Step 5 — Confirm: It summarizes the exact items, quantities and total.
Step 6 — Pay: It generates a secure payment request after customer confirmation.
Step 7 — Verify: The payment integration confirms the transaction.
Step 8 — Operate: Voxy creates the order, updates the dashboard and triggers the next business workflow.
Step 9 — Inform: Customer receives confirmation and the business sees the order.
Later, the owner can ask: “How is business today?” Voxy can summarize sales, order volume, best-selling
products, low-stock items and pending actions from the connected business data.

17. Product Principles
●
 Action over conversation: Voxy should complete useful work, not only chat.
●
 Business-grounded: answers must come from the business's approved information.
●
 Confirmation before commitment: verify important customer details before financial or irreversible actions.
●
 Human when necessary: the AI should escalate rather than guess.
●
 Simple for owners: a small business owner should not need technical knowledge.
●
 Consistent experience: text, voice and future channels should use the same business brain.
●
 Measurable value: track orders, conversion, response time, payments and tasks completed.
18. What Voxy Becomes
The long-term vision is not simply to create a better chatbot. Voxy becomes a layer of AI employees that
businesses can configure and connect to their existing workflows.
Customer-facing: sell, answer, recommend, order and support.
Operations-facing: monitor, organize, alert and summarize.
Transaction-facing: facilitate approved payment workflows.
Voice-facing: communicate naturally through speech.
