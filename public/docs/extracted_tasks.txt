1. Purpose
This document converts the Voxy product direction into small, sequential, assignable engineering tasks. Each developer has a linear stream of work, but the three streams are designed to connect at specific milestones. The goal is to avoid assigning an entire technical area such as “AI” or “backend” to one person as a single massive task.
2. Product Foundation
Voxy is an AI employee for businesses. It is designed to communicate with customers, answer questions, recommend products, sell, confirm orders, facilitate payments, and support business operations. The business side manages products, prices, policies, orders, customers, payments, inventory information, and agent settings. The customer side uses web chat and future voice channels.
The product documentation emphasizes business-grounded answers, confirmation before important actions, human handoff when necessary, and one consistent business brain across text and voice.

4. How the Linear Workflow Works
A developer should normally move to the next task as soon as the previous task is complete enough for integration. The streams do not require one developer to finish all 11 tasks before another developer can use the work.
Core principle: small task → finish → test → hand off/interface → next task.
Example parallel progression:
Abraham: A2 Rebrand → A3 Landing Page → A4 Authentication UI
Samkiel: S2 AI Integration → S3 Business Knowledge → S4 Conversation Engine

9. End-to-End Voxy Flow
1. Business signs up → creates business profile.
2. Business adds products/services, prices, variants, images, availability and policies.
3. Business configures Voxy's personality, rules and permitted actions.
4. Customer opens the business's Voxy link and starts a conversation.
5. Voxy understands intent and retrieves the business's approved information.
6. Voxy recommends products or answers questions using real business data.
7. Customer selects products/services.
8. Voxy builds the draft order and checks relevant information.
9. Voxy summarizes the exact order, quantities and total.
10. Customer explicitly confirms.
11. Voxy requests a secure payment through the connected payment provider.
12. Payment provider verifies the payment and sends the result to Voxy.
13. Backend records the verified transaction and updates the business balance/ledger where the wallet flow is enabled.
14. Order is marked paid/confirmed and the business dashboard updates.
15. Receipt is generated.
16. Customer receives confirmation.
17. Business can view transactions/balance and request withdrawal according to the configured payment/settlement system.
10. Definition of Done for Every Task
Implementation is committed to the shared repository.
Task has been tested locally.
Expected success and error states are handled.
No known broken path from the previous task is introduced.
API/interface assumptions are documented when another developer depends on them.
The next developer can continue without needing to reconstruct the previous developer's work.
Integration points are clearly identified before marking the task complete.

Business operations dashboard

Human handoff
Basic voice interaction if feasible
12. Important Architecture Rules
Voxy must act on approved business information rather than inventing products, prices, stock, discounts, delivery times or policies.
Important or financially significant actions require confirmation before execution.
The AI should have explicit tool permissions. It should not automatically receive permission to perform sensitive actions such as refunds or price changes.
Text and voice should use the same business brain, business data, order tools and payment workflow.
YarnGPT is used as the voice/TTS layer; it is not treated as Voxy's core business reasoning layer.
Payment processing must use a trusted payment provider. The AI should not handle sensitive payment information insecurely.
Every important agent action should be logged so the business can review what happened.
Human handoff must exist for unsupported, ambiguous, sensitive or complex situations.
The virtual-account/wallet/withdrawal system is an added product requirement and must be implemented around the actual capabilities and compliance requirements of the selected payment provider.
