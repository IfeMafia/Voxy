 VOXY VOICE
 AI Voice Employee for Businesses
Purpose: Give businesses a natural AI phone/voice employee that can answer customers,
understand requests, recommend products, take orders and trigger approved business
actions.
1. What Is Voxy Voice?
Voxy Voice is the voice version of the Voxy AI employee. A customer can call a business and speak naturally
instead of typing. Voxy listens, understands the request, responds with a natural voice, and can use the same
business information and tools available to the text-based Voxy agent.
Important: Voxy Voice is not just an AI receptionist. Its purpose is to help complete business tasks — especially
customer service, sales and ordering.
Core abilities

Answer inbound business calls.

Understand natural customer speech.

Answer questions using the business's real information.

Recommend relevant products or services.

Take and confirm orders.

Generate an approved payment request.

Check order/payment status through connected tools.

Transfer or hand off to a human when necessary.

Keep a record of the call and actions taken.
2. What the Business Provides
When a business creates Voxy Voice, it should provide the same core information used by the Voxy AI employee:

Business name and logo

Business description

Products/services

Product images and descriptions

Prices and variants

Opening hours

Delivery/service areas

Payment methods

FAQs and business policies

Human contact/escalation details

3. The Call Experience
The experience should feel like calling a real receptionist or sales representative — short, warm and direct.
Opening example
Voxy: “Good afternoon, welcome to Mama Kitchen's Food. I'm Voxy, your virtual assistant. How can I help you
today?”
If the customer says they want to order
Customer: “I'd like to order food for five people.”
Voxy: “Absolutely. We have a few options for five people. What kind of food are you looking for?”
If the customer asks a question
Customer: “Are you open today?”
Voxy: “Yes, we're open until nine tonight. Would you like to place an order?”
4. How Voxy Should Speak
Voice is different from chat. Customers cannot scroll back through what was said, so Voxy should keep each turn
short and ask one question at a time. This is also consistent with current voice-agent design guidance.

Natural conversation
Voxy should sound conversational rather than like it is reading a script. Small natural phrases such as “Okay, got
you,” “Sure,” “Let me check that,” and “Perfect” can make the interaction feel more natural — but they should not
be overused.
5. Voxy Voice Sales & Order Flow
Customer request → Understand intent → Ask needed question → Recommend → Customer chooses →
Confirm order → Create order → Payment → Verify → Confirm completion

6. How the Voice Agent Works Behind the Scenes
A practical Voxy Voice architecture has four main real-time parts: phone/voice connection, speech-to-text, the AI
reasoning layer, and text-to-speech. A business knowledge layer and business tools sit around them so the agent
can answer from real data and perform actions. ■cite■turn0search5■

Business tools

Voice response

7. Voxy Voice — Core AI Instruction
The voice agent should have a structured system instruction. Current voice-agent guidance recommends defining
identity/personality, response rules, guardrails, context, workflows and examples. ■cite■turn0search0■
Short Voxy Voice prompt concept
Identity: You are Voxy, the AI employee for [Business Name]. You help customers with questions,
recommendations, orders and approved business tasks.
Personality: Friendly, confident, warm and professional. Sound like a helpful Nigerian business representative
when appropriate to the business.
Speaking style: Keep replies short. Ask one question at a time. Listen carefully. Do not give long speeches. Use
natural conversational phrases.
Sales behavior: Understand what the customer needs, recommend the best matching product, answer
objections using business information, and guide the customer toward a purchase without being pushy.
Order behavior: Collect the required order details, repeat the final order and total, and ask the customer to
confirm before creating the final order or initiating payment.
Truthfulness: Never invent prices, products, availability, discounts or payment status. Use connected tools for
information that must be accurate.
Human handoff: If the request is outside your capabilities or the customer asks for a human, transfer/escalate
according to the business's rules.
8. Key Conversation Scenarios
A. General enquiry
“Hi, welcome to [Business Name]. I'm Voxy. How can I help you today?”
B. Product enquiry
“Yes, we have that available. It's [price]. Would you like me to tell you a little more about it?”
C. Recommendation
“If you're looking for something affordable for four people, I'd recommend [product]. Would you like to order it?”
D. Order confirmation
“Just to confirm, that's two [product], one [add-on], for a total of [amount]. Is that correct?”
E. Payment
“Perfect. I'll prepare your payment option now.” Then confirm payment only after the payment system reports
success.
F. Human handoff
“I want to make sure you get the right help. Let me connect you with someone from the team.”
